# Third-party files

- `lib/vexflow-core.js` — VexFlow 5 (MIT License), https://github.com/vexflow/vexflow
- `fonts/bravura.woff2` — Bravura music font by Steinberg (SIL Open Font License), via @vexflow-fonts/bravura
- `fonts/academico.woff2` — Academico text font (SIL Open Font License), via @vexflow-fonts/academico
- `piano/*.mp3` — Acoustic grand piano samples from the FluidR3 GM soundfont, as packaged in gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
